This Act hereby establishes the national symbols of the Kingdom of Zatsunei, including their standards and protections.
<br><br>
<p align="right">The Kingdom of Zatsunei</p> 
<hr>
</div>

<br>

## Chapter One ― General Provisions

### § 1. Purpose and scope
**1.** The purpose of this Act is to establish the official national symbols which are not otherwise provided by law. Regulations, standards, and protections shall be set hereinafter for national symbols.

**2.** This Act shall apply jurisdiction to all federal territories of the Kingdom of Zatsunei and shall enter into force on the day of its promulgation.

### § 2. Definitions
**1.** "National symbol" means any flag, emblem, coat of arms, or other visual representation recognized by the Kingdom as a national symbol.

**2.** "Commercial use" means any use of a national symbol, or a representation of it, in advertising, marketing, merchandising, or sale of goods or services for profit.

### § 3. Recognition of national symbols
A national symbol shall be recognized either by Royal Decree or upon adoption into this Act.

### § 4. Official access to national symbols
The folder `/assets/` shall serve as an official repository for all digital representations of national symbols established in this Act.

<br>

## Chapter Two ― National flag

### § 5. The national flag
**1.** The national flag of the Kingdom of Zatsunei, also referred to as the Zatsuni flag, shall have a proportion of 9:7 (width to height) and consist of two vertical fields of equal width. The field on the hoist side shall be magenta (RGB: 202, 31, 123), and the field on the fly side shall be pure white (RGB: 255, 255, 255). The national emblem shall be positioned at the exact center of the flag and shall be rendered in a darker gold (RGB: 194, 128, 29) on the magenta field of the hoist, and a lighter gold (RGB: 210, 151, 24) on the white field of the fly. The national emblem shall take around ⅐ of the national flag in size.

### § 6. Official usage and display of the national flag
The national flag shall be displayed at full mast on all properties of any government under the Kingdom of Zatsunei, embassies of the Kingdom of Zatsunei, and during official ceremonies, unless otherwise provided.

### § 7. Respect and protection for the national flag
**1.** The national flag of the Kingdom of Zatsunei shall be treated with respect and care. No person shall:
 - (a) lay the flag on the ground or water;
 - (b) soil, damage, distort, deface, or destroy the flag; or
 - (c) display the flag in a manner that obscures, inverts, or otherwise degrades its form or symbolic significance.

**2.** The national flag may be displayed in an inverted orientation as a signal of immediate distress in instances of danger to life or property.

### § 8. Usage of the national flag for commercial purposes
No person shall use the national flag for commercial purposes in a manner that misrepresents, disrespects, or exploits its symbolic significance.

### § 9. Retirement, replacement, and maintenance of the national flag
The national flag shall be maintained. Upon damage to the flag, it shall be replaced with a new flag that conforms to the regulations of this Act. The damaged flag shall be retired in a dignified manner, either by burial or storage in a clean location.

### § 10. Usage of a Minecraft banner as an alternative to the national flag
**1.** In Minecraft, a banner may be used as an alternative to the national flag when there is no method available to properly represent the national flag in its original design.

**2.** The banner shall consist of two vertical fields of equal width, with the hoist field colored in magenta dye and the fly field colored in white dye.
